Frequently Asked Questions
What markets are available for American football games?
For single games: Winner, Spread, Total, 1st Half (Winner/Spread/Total), Team Totals, and Player Props. Futures include: Playoffs, Win Total, Conference Winner, Division Winner, Champion, and Season Awards (MVP, DROY, OROY, DPOY, OPOY).
What markets are available for baseball games?
For MLB single games: Winner, Spread, Total, 1st Inning Totals (YRFI/NRFI), First 5 Innings (Winner/Spread/Total), Team Totals, Batter Props (HR, Hits, TB, RBI, Runs, HRR, Strikeouts, Stolen Bases), and Pitcher Props (Ks, Hits Allowed, ER Allowed, BB Allowed). Futures include: Playoffs, Win Total, League Winner, Division Winner, Champion, and Season Awards.
What markets are available for basketball games?
For NBA single games: Winner, Spread, Total, 1st Half (Winner/Spread/Total), Team Totals, and Player Props (First Basket, Points, Rebounds, Assists, 3PM, and combo markets like PRA, PR, PA, RA, Double-Double). Futures include: Playoffs, Win Total, Champion, and Season Awards (MVP, DPOY).
What markets are available for golf?
PGA single-event markets include Round and Tournament Matchups (not available for live trading). Futures include Tournament Winner and Top 5/10/20 Finish.
What markets are available for professional hockey?
Single game markets: Winner, Spread, Total, and Player Props (Goals, Assists). Futures include To Make the Playoffs.
What markets are available for Tennis?
Single match markets: Winner, Game Spread, Total Games. Set markets (not available for live trading): Set Spread, Total Sets, First Set Winner. Futures include Tournament Winner.
What markets are available for MMA?
Single fight markets: Winner, Total Rounds, and To Go the Distance.
Is live trading available for all sports?
Most sports support live (in-play) trading. Check the app to see what's available at any given moment!
