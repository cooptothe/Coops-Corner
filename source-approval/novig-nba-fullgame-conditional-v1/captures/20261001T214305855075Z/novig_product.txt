Novig operates as a Designation Contract Market (DCM) and must comply with applicable federal, state, and local laws. Participants are also responsible for ensuring their own compliance with local regulations.
Frequently Asked Questions
Are there legal restrictions on participating?
Yes. Participants must comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws. The exchange is open to users who are 21 or older and physically located in the United States. Novig does not currently operate in Michigan, Nevada, and Arizona.
How does Novig handle legal disputes?
Legal disputes are governed by Novig's
Member Agreement
, which includes the applicable dispute resolution process. Please refer to the full Terms of Use for details.
Where can I find the official Rulebook and Member Agreement?
Both documents are available in the Help Center under the Policies collection, along with many other policy documents. Always refer to the most current version as the terms are subject to change.
